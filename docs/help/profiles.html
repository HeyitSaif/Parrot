Call profiles

A sales discovery call and a 1:1 with a teammate need very different help. Profiles are how you tell the Copilot which kind of call this is. You pick one with the chips above the record button; Parrot ships with six to start (Default, Sales discovery, 1:1 coaching, Interview and friends) and you can edit all of them or build your own in Settings → Profiles.

What a profile controls

  • Kinds are the card types the Copilot watches for. Each has a name, an icon, a color, and a plain-English trigger like "they raised a concern about price". Mark a kind as pinned and its cards stick to the top until handled, which is what makes blockers feel like blockers.
  • Gauges are the little meters in the panel, like buying temperature on the sales profile. They're scored fresh on every pass.
  • Persona and tone tell the model who you are in the call and how to phrase its advice.

The one-line brief

Next to the profile chips there's a text field for a one-line brief, like "renewal call with Acme, they're unhappy about the migration". It's optional, takes five seconds, and noticeably sharpens the first minutes of a call because the Copilot doesn't have to figure out the context from scratch.
